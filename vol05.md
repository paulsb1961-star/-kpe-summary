### Description

When shadows fall across the snow blossoms, the hill holds its breath.

A quiet photographic meditation set at Yongchuljeong Pavilion, the symbol of Eunpyeong Hanok Village beneath Bukhansan in Seoul. This is not a guide to Korea. It is a way of feeling it.

Through HongSahn's lens, snow gathers like blossom across the open field and along the pavilion's lines. White layers the railings and steps; the ridges of Bukhansan rise pale behind. Light shifts slowly over the snowfield, and the pavilion stands as a single quiet form between earth and winter sky.

Yongchuljeong rises as the symbol of Eunpyeong Hanok Village, where the ridgelines of Bukhansan meet the rooftops below. Under snow the hill becomes a field of soft blossoms, and the pavilion — built for pause and prospect — turns into a place to measure stillness rather than distance.

The Korean Photo Essay Series (KPE Archive) is a long-form photographic archive documenting emotional time across Korean landscapes. Season I: Coming and Going Time comprises 30 individual photo essays and 10 thematic collections, 2026.

This volume continues the winter beneath Bukhansan, rising from the village to the snow-covered hill.
