### Description

Beyond the pond, spring blossoms and autumn leaves arrive in turn, and new faces pass between the gates.

A quiet photographic meditation set in Deoksugung Palace — a Joseon palace at the center of modern Seoul. This is not a guide to Korea. It is a way of feeling it.

Through HongSahn's lens, the seasons turn within the old walls. Blossoms give way to leaves; shadows lengthen and recede; people of spring and autumn pass through the palace gates, each carrying their own quiet time.

Five minutes from City Hall, where glass towers rise and traffic moves, Deoksugung keeps history at walking distance. Its blend of palace halls and early-modern architecture makes it a threshold between eras — a place where the past and the present meet beneath the same sky.

The Korean Photo Essay Series (KPE Archive) is a long-form photographic archive documenting emotional time across Korean landscapes. Season I: Coming and Going Time comprises 30 individual photo essays and 10 thematic collections, 2026.

“This is not a guide to Korea. It is a way of feeling it.”

This volume lingers where palace seasons turn, among the people of spring and autumn.
