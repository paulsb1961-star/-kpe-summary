### Description

When autumn dyes the slopes of Bukhansan, everyone climbing the path becomes a familiar companion.

A quiet photographic meditation set at Daeseomun, the Great Western Gate of Bukhansanseong Fortress near Seoul. This is not a guide to Korea. It is a way of feeling it.

Through HongSahn's lens, maple leaves flow down the mountain. Color gathers along the fortress walls; climbers pass the old gate in clear autumn light; strangers nod like companions, carried together by the season.

Daeseomun, the Great Western Gate, is the main entrance to Bukhansanseong Fortress — a stone threshold where the mountain's history meets its walkers. In autumn the slopes turn many-hued, and the gate stands quietly as the leaves and the people flow through.

The Korean Photo Essay Archive is a long-form photographic archive documenting emotional time across Korean landscapes. Season I: Coming and Going Time comprises 30 individual photo essays and 10 thematic collections, 2026.

This volume climbs to a fortress gate, where autumn leaves flow and strangers walk as companions.
