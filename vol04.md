### Description

On the day the first snow falls, the village grows even quieter.

A quiet photographic meditation set in Eunpyeong Hanok Village, beneath Bukhansan in Seoul. This is not a guide to Korea. It is a way of feeling it.

Through HongSahn's lens, winter settles over the tiled roofs one flake at a time. The first snow softens the lanes, the hanok eaves carry a thin white line, and the village seems to hold its breath. Each frame lingers on the hush between falling and fallen — the moment a place becomes still.

At the foot of Bukhansan in Jingwan-dong, Eunpyeong Hanok Village gathers traditional Korean houses against the mountain's ridgelines. In snow it sheds its postcard brightness and turns inward — courtyards, walls, and rooflines reduced to quiet shapes, a contemporary village remembering an older way of being still.

The Korean Photo Essay Archive is a long-form photographic archive documenting emotional time across Korean landscapes. Season I: Coming and Going Time comprises 30 individual photo essays and 10 thematic collections, 2026.

This volume opens the winter sequence beneath Bukhansan, where snow becomes a kind of silence.
