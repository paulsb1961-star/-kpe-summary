### Description

As snow begins to fall, the world grows quiet, and I walk along the path beneath the hill.

A quiet photographic meditation set in Gijachon Village, beneath Bukhansan in Jingwan-dong, Seoul. This is not a guide to Korea. It is a way of feeling it.

Through HongSahn's lens, a single snow path leads beneath the hill and into stillness. Footprints, fences, and bare branches gather under falling snow; the slope rises softly into white. Each frame follows the quiet of walking — the way a familiar place turns unfamiliar, and tender, under the first heavy snow.

Nestled beneath Bukhansan in Jingwan-dong, Gijachon was once home to journalists and has slowly faded at the city's edge. In snow its lanes and low houses become a path of memory — a vanishing village held for a moment in white, walked rather than viewed.

The Korean Photo Essay Series (KPE Archive) is a long-form photographic archive documenting emotional time across Korean landscapes. Season I: Coming and Going Time comprises 30 individual photo essays and 10 thematic collections, 2026.

This volume closes the first winter cluster beneath Bukhansan, following a single path into the snow.
