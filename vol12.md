### Description

I used to look broadly; now I look through a narrow gap, and the near and the far come together as one.

A quiet photographic meditation set along the road to Bulgwangsa and Bulgwang Neighborhood Park, beneath Bukhansan in Seoul. This is not a guide to Korea. It is a way of feeling it.

Through HongSahn's lens, walking slows until distance dissolves. Through the narrowest gap, nearness and farness quietly meet; vague memories appear gently; the fruit of time grown in indifference is seen anew. Each frame is a step taken without hurry.

At the foot of Bukhansan, in the quiet folds of Bulgwang-dong, the road climbs toward Bulgwangsa, beside Bulgwang Neighborhood Park where legend and stillness share a single hill. Here a slow walk becomes its own kind of seeing.

The Korean Photo Essay Series (KPE Archive) is a long-form photographic archive documenting emotional time across Korean landscapes. Season I: Coming and Going Time comprises 30 individual photo essays and 10 thematic collections, 2026.

This volume slows to a walking pace, where steps moving slowly bring the near and the far into one.
