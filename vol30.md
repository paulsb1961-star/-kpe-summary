### Description

When flowers fall and summer passes, autumn leaves arrive, and even the palace pond deepens into fall.

A quiet photographic meditation set at Chundangji Pond in Changgyeonggung Palace, in the heart of Seoul. This is not a guide to Korea. It is a way of feeling it.

Through HongSahn's lens, a pond remembers the year. Leaves drift onto still water; reflections darken and glow; the old halls stand mirrored in the deepening surface as autumn settles over Chundangji.

Deep within Changgyeonggung Palace, Chundangji lies like a pond that remembers — once royal rice fields, later a still water garden. As the seasons turn, its surface gathers blossom and leaf alike, holding the palace's long autumns in quiet reflection.

The Korean Photo Essay Series (KPE Archive) is a long-form photographic archive documenting emotional time across Korean landscapes. Season I: Coming and Going Time comprises 30 individual photo essays and 10 thematic collections, 2026.

“This is not a guide to Korea. It is a way of feeling it.”

This volume closes Season I at a palace pond, where autumn leaves rest on water that remembers.
