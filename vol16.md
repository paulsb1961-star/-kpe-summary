### Description

When spring sunlight settles on the leaves, the pond is embroidered with petals of many colors.

A quiet photographic meditation set in Changgyeonggung Palace — a Joseon royal palace in the heart of Seoul. This is not a guide to Korea. It is a way of feeling it.

Through HongSahn's lens, shadow and sunlight share the spring. Petals drift onto still water; the laughter of visitors passes lightly between old halls; light moves slowly across the courtyards, embroidering the day.

Among Seoul's palaces, Changgyeonggung feels closest to spring. Built as a residence for royal elders, it carries a gentler intimacy than the grander palaces — and when spring fully blooms, its ponds and pavilions soften into color and shade.

The Korean Photo Essay Series (KPE Archive) is a long-form photographic archive documenting emotional time across Korean landscapes. Season I: Coming and Going Time comprises 30 individual photo essays and 10 thematic collections, 2026.

“This is not a guide to Korea. It is a way of feeling it.”

This volume returns to the palaces, where spring sunlight casts its quiet shadows.
