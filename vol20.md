### Description

On a languid afternoon bathed in blue sky, even time itself becomes a shadow.

A quiet photographic meditation set at the National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art in Gwacheon, at the foot of the mountain near Seoul. This is not a guide to Korea. It is a way of feeling it.

Through HongSahn's lens, architecture and sky reflect one another. Glass and stone gather the blue overhead; figures cross open plazas; a photograph taken twenty years ago surfaces again, and the present folds gently into memory.

At the foot of Gwacheon's mountain, the museum breathes with the landscape around it — its architecture rising and resting with the slope. Here art, building, and sky meet, and a slow afternoon becomes a quiet meditation on reflection and time.

The Korean Photo Essay Series (KPE Archive) is a long-form photographic archive documenting emotional time across Korean landscapes. Season I: Coming and Going Time comprises 30 individual photo essays and 10 thematic collections, 2026.

“This is not a guide to Korea. It is a way of feeling it.”

This volume pauses where architecture breathes with the mountain, beneath a reflecting sky.
