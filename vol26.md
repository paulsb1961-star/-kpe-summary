### Description

When the blue sky settles over the wide fields, even adults walk with a child's fluttering heart.

A quiet photographic meditation set in Haneul (Sky) Park, on the heights above the Han River in Seoul. This is not a guide to Korea. It is a way of feeling it.

Through HongSahn's lens, time comes and goes across open grass. Silver eulalia waves in the wind; bright laughter drifts over the fields; the wide sky settles low, and the day moves with the lightness of children at play.

Sky Park rises where Nanjido once held a vast landfill — land reclaimed and reborn as meadow for the 2002 World Cup. Now grass and wind have erased the old scars, and the high field becomes a place where time, coming and going, is felt as open air.

The Korean Photo Essay Archive is a long-form photographic archive documenting emotional time across Korean landscapes. Season I: Coming and Going Time comprises 30 individual photo essays and 10 thematic collections, 2026.

“This is not a guide to Korea. It is a way of feeling it.”

This volume rises to a sky-high meadow, where time keeps coming and going on the wind.
