### Description

On the island where immortals were said to wander, a passerby pauses her steps.

A quiet photographic meditation set in Seonyudo Park, on the wide breathing of the Han River in Seoul. This is not a guide to Korea. It is a way of feeling it.

Through HongSahn's lens, water and concrete learn to rest. Vines climb the old tanks; reflections gather in still basins; a passerby pauses, youth whispers nearby, and the island holds many ages of life at once.

Once a water treatment plant, Seonyudo was reborn as a garden where industry softened into green. Along the river's slow breath, it rests like a quiet pause in the city — a place where immortals were once imagined to play, now open to anyone walking slowly.

The Korean Photo Essay Series (KPE Archive) is a long-form photographic archive documenting emotional time across Korean landscapes. Season I: Coming and Going Time comprises 30 individual photo essays and 10 thematic collections, 2026.

This volume drifts to a river island where water learned, at last, to rest.
