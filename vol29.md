### Description

Leaving the city behind, I walk up the valley, and the sound of flowing water lightens each step.

A quiet photographic meditation set at Geumseonsa, high on the slopes of Bukhansan in Seoul. This is not a guide to Korea. It is a way of feeling it.

Through HongSahn's lens, the mountain breathes. Water falls away down the valley while pilgrims climb; pine scent thickens; a small temple appears where concrete fades into stone, and each step grows lighter as the city sinks below.

High above the layered streets of Seoul, where concrete gives way to pine, Geumseonsa keeps the mountain's quiet. Reached only by climbing, it rewards the slow walker with stillness — a place where ascending the valley becomes its own kind of prayer.

The Korean Photo Essay Series (KPE Archive) is a long-form photographic archive documenting emotional time across Korean landscapes. Season I: Coming and Going Time comprises 30 individual photo essays and 10 thematic collections, 2026.

This volume climbs the valley to a mountain temple, where the slope itself breathes.
