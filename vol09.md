### Description

A hanok village where past and present stand side by side, and three philosophies once shared a single hillside.

A quiet photographic meditation set in Eunpyeong Hanok Village and Jegakmal, beneath Bukhansan in Seoul. This is not a guide to Korea. It is a way of feeling it.

Through HongSahn's lens, the past and the present rest together. On one patch of grass lies a descendant of a king; across the stream, Buddhist prayer rises on the mountain slope. Tiled roofs hold the memory of filial devotion while today's village goes quietly about its day.

Before the tiled roofs of today's Eunpyeong Hanok Village, this land was called Jegangmal, named for royal ancestral shrines once built here. The teachings of Confucianism, Buddhism, and Taoism were passed down together — and their traces still linger where past and present coexist.

The Korean Photo Essay Archive is a long-form photographic archive documenting emotional time across Korean landscapes. Season I: Coming and Going Time comprises 30 individual photo essays and 10 thematic collections, 2026.

This volume lingers where the village's past and present keep each other company.
