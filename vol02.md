### Description

Autumn rain settles over the Secret Garden, and the palace breathes more slowly.

A quiet photographic meditation set in Changdeokgung Palace — a UNESCO World Heritage Site in the heart of Seoul. This is not a guide to Korea. It is a way of feeling it.

Through HongSahn's lens, rain becomes its own language. Drops gather on the still face of Buyongji, streams gain strength along the eaves, and the zelkova stands darkened and patient. Mallards drift across a soaked pond while visitors move beneath a grey, low sky — every surface softened, every sound hushed.

Tucked along the slope of Bugaksan, the Secret Garden of Changdeokgung was made for stillness rather than display. In the rain its pavilions and ponds turn inward, and the garden reveals a different season of feeling: Buyongji under a steady shower, Yeonghwadang in the wet, and Aeryeonji veiled by falling water.

The Korean Photo Essay Series (KPE Archive) is a long-form photographic archive documenting emotional time across Korean landscapes. Season I: Coming and Going Time comprises 30 individual photo essays and 10 thematic collections, 2026.

This volume continues the Secret Garden sequence — the same place, seen through the weather of memory.
