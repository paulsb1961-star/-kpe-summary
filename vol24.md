### Description

The children shout, and beneath the blue sky they hurl their untainted hearts into the world.

A quiet photographic meditation set in Yongsan, around the National Museum of Korea in Seoul. This is not a guide to Korea. It is a way of feeling it.

Through HongSahn's lens, echoes move across open ground. Children run and call out; their voices scatter beneath wide reflecting pools and tall stone halls; the present brims with life while history stands quietly behind it.

In the heart of Yongsan, the National Museum of Korea is more than a keeper of relics — its broad plazas and water mirror the city around it. Here the long past and the bright present share the same ground, and a child's shout carries where echoes once went.

The Korean Photo Essay Archive is a long-form photographic archive documenting emotional time across Korean landscapes. Season I: Coming and Going Time comprises 30 individual photo essays and 10 thematic collections, 2026.

This volume listens where the echoes went, as the present plays before the past.
