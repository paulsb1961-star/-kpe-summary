### Description

From high above the apartments, I watched the traces of the departed scatter along the stream.

A quiet photographic meditation set along Gongneungcheon, on the northern edge of Seoul. This is not a guide to Korea. It is a way of feeling it.

Through HongSahn's lens, a stream becomes a river of time. Walking paths, railway lines, and quiet water carry memory southward. Seen from above, the everyday turns tender — a place to walk a while with someone who has gone before, and to glimpse, in the ordinary, the back of a feeling once held.

Gongneungcheon rises in the hills of Goyang and moves south through the northern edge of the city before joining the Han River. Along its banks lie footpaths and old railway traces — an ordinary waterway that, walked slowly, becomes a passage through memory.

The Korean Photo Essay Series (KPE Archive) is a long-form photographic archive documenting emotional time across Korean landscapes. Season I: Coming and Going Time comprises 30 individual photo essays and 10 thematic collections, 2026.

This volume follows the water beyond the forested railway, where memory crosses the river of time.
